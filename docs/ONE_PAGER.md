# AliveLine — an AI planner that tells students when to start

1. **Team.** Artem Serhiichuk, Ostap Sukhomlyn, Andrii Antoniuk.
2. **Product and goal.** AliveLine is a web service that collects a student's deadlines from all courses, estimates the workload of each assignment in hours from its description, and tells the student when to start. Pain removed: students see a deadline but do not know how much time the task needs, so they postpone work until the last night. Moodle, Google Calendar and Todoist remind about the submission date (the deadline). In contrast, AliveLine gives not the submission date but the date when work should begin. Goal: students start on time instead of the night before the deadline.
3. **Users.** First- and second-year students of the Ukrainian Catholic University (UCU) who take 4-6 courses at once, with deadlines in Moodle. Our first audience is our own year group and our dorms, which we can reach directly through their group chats.
4. **Promotion and validation.** How we reach users: we post in the chats of our courses, our year group and our dorms, and hold five short interviews with students about how they plan deadlines today. How we check that they want it (time and money, not words like "great idea"): an early-access signup form with an email (target: 20 signups in 7 days); a first working version that students use and return to (target: 8 students who come back, where a return is a second login on a different day); and a request to donate from 20 UAH to a volunteer cause after testing. If results fall below the targets, we change the problem or the audience.
5. **First-version features.** Sign-up and sign-in; adding assignments manually, by importing a Moodle calendar (.ics) file, or automatically via the Moodle API if the university allows it; an AI estimate of the time needed, based on the assignment description, which the student can correct; a work window for each assignment (a start date and a personal target finish date, both counted back from the real deadline across all assignments), with the statuses "can wait", "time to start" and "critical". Notifications and a mobile app are outside the first version.
